A family firm that still answers its own phone

Whitfield Plumbing & Heating was founded in 1994 by Ray Whitfield, working out of a van and a small workshop on Foundry Lane. Today the business is run day-to-day by his son Danny, with the same approach Ray started with: turn up when you say you will, explain the job in plain English, and charge a fair price for it.

Over thirty years on, we're still based in Ashcombe, still family-run, and still doing the unglamorous work that keeps a house warm and its water hot — properly, and without the upsell.

  • Gas Safe registered engineers on every job
  • Fully insured for domestic & light commercial work
  • Fixed, upfront quotes before any work begins
  • Local to Ashcombe and the surrounding villages
